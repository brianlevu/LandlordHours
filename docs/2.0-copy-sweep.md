## Left on purpose

- Stored values: category names (`Repairs & Maintenance` and the rest), filing statuses (`Married, Joint`, `Qualifying Widow(er)`), and `STR (Short-Term Rental)` are saved in user data and synced through iCloud. Renaming them needs a migration plus a separate display name. Proposed as a follow-up.
- Onboarding, House and the Today hero: mechanical fixes only (apostrophes). The owner is rethinking that messaging in a separate lane.
- Learning center article bodies, legal text, `PaywallView.swift`, the restore-purchases section of `SettingsView.swift`, and `Services/Capture/Classifier/*` (owned by other lanes).
- Prompts and field descriptions sent to the on-device model (`NoteReading`, `NoteModelBackends`): not shown to people, so their straight apostrophes stay.
- IRS terms the gate marks as jargon (`Filing status`, `Grouping election`, `100-hour test`, `Material participation`): they name the real thing on a tax form. Rewording them would hide what the user is tracking.
- Proper names and sample data (`Private Cloud Compute`, `Pine Cabin`, `Oak Street`, `Sample Cabin`).
- Log and debug strings (`Entry load failed...`, `Suggestion store unreadable...`) and regexes: never shown.
- `Unlock Pro`: the one consistent unlock action across the app.
