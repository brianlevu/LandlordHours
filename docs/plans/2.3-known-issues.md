# 2.3 known issues, deferred to 2.3.1

Brian decided on 2026-11-02 to ship 2.3 with minor issues and fix them in 2.3.1. None of these is a crash, data loss, a purchase defect or a Release launch flag. Evidence for the fix lane: `~/AgentScratch/landlordhours-fix-rel23/2026-11-01/` and the verifier's `~/AgentScratch/landlordhours-fix23-verify/2026-11-02/`.

## 1. Today's Undo toast drops to the bottom after a scrolled Today, and the chip can stay hidden

Visual gate on `fix/2.3-release-gate` dd6f8d2 (frames `frames-undo/`, iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, light): after Not rental work with Today scrolled, the "Marked not rental work · Undo" toast is the opaque bottom card (#FFFFFF, y 2172–2334 px, frames t001136-55 to t001139-58), not the top glass capsule. At scroll offset 0 (t001140-59) the art has stepped down 42 px for a top toast, but the toast is still drawn at the bottom over the first "To confirm" row. After Undo (t001141-60 to t001142-62, two byte-identical frames 0.89 s apart) the rental chip does not come back; the chip area differs from the pre-action frame t001126-44 at x 60–373, y 360–468 px. The code is older than this branch (`LHToast.swift`, `LHToastChipSlot` unchanged). The gate's guess, not checked: `hidesHeroChip` stays true because `current` is not cleared after the claim flips back to `.top`.

## 2. Today's Undo reverses the inbox's last action, not its own

Today's toast calls `inbox.undo()` (`DashboardView.swift` lines 511 and 671), which reverses whatever the inbox model did last. Toasts queue one at a time, so two quick Today actions could undo the wrong one, or do nothing on a queued toast. Inferred by the code verifier, not reproduced.

## 3. Action-less Inbox toasts now stay 8 s, not 4 s

The inbox's undo window now matches the toast's 8 s action hold (standard §1.19), so Undo works for the whole time it shows. Inbox toasts with no action ("Kept in your inbox", "Already in your records") also use that window, against §1.19's 4 s (it was 5 s before).
