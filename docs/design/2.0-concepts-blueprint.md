# LandlordHours 2.0, round 3: three big ideas

2026-10-18. Design direction only. The baseline anatomy (large titles, confirm-first inbox, status on words, sober PDF) is kept; everything the eye lands on changes. Refs: `refs/REFS.md` (30 Mobbin screens).

Shared laws: no faces or mascots; a graphic never shows an unconfirmed hour (suggestions render hollow or hatched); milestones celebrate honest records, never quantity; every celebration runs under 3s with Continue at the top; the PDF never carries the illustration. Intent colours in all three: action violet `#5B4CDB` / dark `#A99BFF`; success `#1E9E5A` / `#4ED48A`; caution `#C9741A` / `#FFB454`; destructive `#C8323C` / `#FF6B6B`. They appear only on words, glyphs and buttons, never as fills on illustration. Springs are SwiftUI `.spring(response:dampingFraction:)`; haptics via `.sensoryFeedback`.

---

## 1. Blueprint: your year builds the house

**Big idea.** Every confirmed hour builds one house, drawn first as a blueprint and made real from the ground up. 100h pours the foundation (MP test 3), 500h puts the roof on (MP test 1), 750h lights the windows and hangs the keys on the door (REPS). The unbuilt part stays visible as cyan linework, so "514h to go" is the storeys you have not built. Landlords read buildings; a tax threshold becomes a construction stage.

**Colour.** Blueprint blue is the hero surface in both modes: one unforgettable colour.

Rules: violet lives only on white/dark cards; on blueprint the primary button is a white pill with blueprint text. Categories use a materials list (timber, terracotta, plaster, slate `#5A6B8C`, copper `#B86A3F`, moss `#4E8A5C`, cobalt `#3B6FE0`, plum `#7A4BAE`, sand `#D8B98A`, graphite `#4A5068`, pine `#2E6B4F`) as 12% washes behind row icons; all pass 4.5:1 as text on white.

**Illustration.** Soft-3D architectural render, matte materials, one 3/4 camera (`refs/airbnb-1` is the bar). One house in two states, linework and built, shipped as layered PNGs so SwiftUI reveals the built layer with a mask that rises with hours.

Assets: house in 5 stages (blueprint, foundation, frame, roofed, lit); 4 property types (single, duplex, cabin, small apartment block) at 96pt; 11 category tools on a drafting table (wrench, level, roller, key, phone, ledger, car, clipboard, folder, box, hard hat); empty states (empty lot with a surveyor's stake; empty mailbox; rolled blueprint); Welcome (house half built); brass plaques 100 / 500 / 750.

fal.ai `fal-ai/flux-pro/v1.1-ultra`, 1:1, seeds `41100` house family, `41200` tools, `41300` plaques; `fal-ai/birefnet` cutouts. Style prefix: *"soft 3D architectural render, matte materials, isometric three-quarter view from the front-left, warm studio key light from top-left, gentle ambient occlusion, pale blue-grey drafting paper background, no text, no labels, no people, no characters, clean edges, product-shot framing, centered"*.
- Built house: prefix + *"a small two-storey rental house with a terracotta roof, cream plaster walls, warm timber porch, slate window frames, a brass key hanging on the front door"*.
- Blueprint and stages: image-to-image from the built master, strength 0.55, + *"drawn only as thin cyan technical linework on deep blue blueprint paper, wireframe, no fills"* or *"only the concrete slab and timber frame built, upper storeys as cyan linework"*.
- Tools: prefix + *"a single matte [wrench] lying on a blueprint, small, object only"*. Plaques: prefix + *"a small brass plaque engraved with the number [100], brushed, slight bevel, isolated"*.
Rules: one camera and light sentence; object height 70% of canvas; reject any render whose roof pitch or window count differs from the master.

## 2. Porchlight: your street, lit by your hours

**Big idea.** Your properties stand on one illustrated street. Work at a property lights its windows; every 50 confirmed hours lights one of fifteen street lamps, so the street is fully lit at 750h, with taller lamps at 100 and 500. The sky follows the clock and the ground follows the season, so March at noon and November at 9pm look different, the way the job does.

**Colour.** The sky is the system. Light mode uses the bright skies, dark mode the dark ones, so chrome contrast is always right.

| Sky | Top to horizon | When | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | `#F9A86E` to `#FBE3D0` | light, 05 to 10 | ink 11.8:1 |
| Day | `#4FA8F0` to `#D9EEFF` | light, 10 to 18 | ink 6.9:1 at top |
| Dusk | `#4B2E7F` to `#F07A5A` | dark, 16 to 21 | white 8.6:1 at top |
| Night | `#0C1738` to `#263468` | dark, 21 to 05 | white 15:1 |

Property colours are house paints: brick `#D95F43`, cobalt `#3C7DD9`, pine `#2E8B6B`, ochre `#C29A3B`, plum `#8A4FBF`, slate `#4A6A8F`. Categories use the round-2 table. "On pace" is green text, never a green sky.

**Illustration.** Flat vector with paper grain, two-tone cel shading, long soft shadows (`refs/headspace-1`, `refs/gentlerstreak-1`). Scenes are assembled in SwiftUI from parts so lamps and windows animate: sky (code), 4 seasonal ground strips, 4 house types recoloured in code, 15 lamps (code), seasonal trees.

fal.ai `fal-ai/flux-pro/v1.1-ultra`, seeds `52100` houses, `52200` ground and trees, `52300` spot objects; `fal-ai/birefnet` cutouts; vectorise keepers (6-colour trace) so paints swap in code. Style prefix: *"flat vector illustration with subtle paper grain, two-tone cel shading, long soft shadow to the right, muted saturated palette, straight-on elevation view, isolated on plain white, no text, no people, no characters, no faces, clean geometric shapes, calm editorial quality"*.

- Ground: prefix + *"a flat strip of ground and sidewalk with [spring tulips / summer grass / fallen leaves / snow and bare hedges], seamless left to right"*, 21:9. Trees: prefix + *"a single round maple in [season]"*.
- Spot objects: prefix + *"a [mailbox on a post / wall calendar / paper receipt / car key]"*. Milestone: prefix + *"a tall cast-iron street lamp, lit, warm halo, night"*.
Rules: one elevation angle; windows always 2x2 so lighting is a code mask; no gradients inside the art; reject anything with people or faces in windows.

## 3. Tilework: 750 tiles, one per hour

**Big idea.** The year is a mosaic of exactly 750 tiles, 30 across and 25 down. Each confirmed hour sets one glazed tile in its category colour; unconfirmed hours are empty outlines. Rows 4, 17 and 25 are gold-grouted thresholds (100, 500, 750). A landlord, a CPA and a spouse read it the same way, and nothing can show that was not laid.

**Colour.** Deep grout in both modes so the glazes glow; the rest of the app is quiet plaster.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| canvas | `#F5F2EC` | `#0F1220` | page |
| card | `#FFFFFF` | `#1C2136` | rows, metrics |
| grout | `#161B2E` | same | hero, Welcome, milestone; white 15:1 |
| empty tile | white at 12% outline | same | unlaid hours |
| gold grout | `#E3B64B` | same | threshold rows, medallions; 7.9:1 on grout |
| ink | `#1A1D2B` | `#F3F2EE` | text |

Glazes on grout (all 4.5:1 or better, re-measured in the render step): repairs `#F0744A`, renovation `#F2C230`, turnover `#3FC8E6`, leasing `#D56BE0`, tenant comms `#4F8BFF`, bookkeeping `#A4D64A`, travel `#2FD1B8`, inspections `#8FA6D6`, legal `#C9CFE0`, supplies `#E5A06E`, management `#5CD68A`. Spouse tiles use the same glaze with a corner notch, so the Who split needs no second hue. Success green is never a glaze.

**Illustration.** Glazed ceramic, soft 3D, visible bevel, on a dark grout slab (`refs/monzo-2`, `refs/notboring-2`). The mosaic itself is code. Assets: 11 category tiles with an embossed tool; 4 property tiles (house silhouettes); 3 gold medallions; empty states (trowel and a stack of blank tiles; a cracked blank tile for "needs hours"); Welcome (a half-finished wall mosaic of a house).

fal.ai `fal-ai/flux-pro/v1.1-ultra`, seeds `63100` tiles, `63200` medallions, `63300` scenes; `fal-ai/birefnet` cutouts. Style prefix: *"soft 3D render of a glazed ceramic tile, square with a slight bevel and a subtle gloss highlight at the top-left, matte dark charcoal grout background, studio light from top-left, shallow depth, no text, no people, no characters, centered, product-shot framing"*.
- Category tile: prefix + *"the tile is [coral orange] glaze with a [wrench] embossed into its surface, one tone darker in the recess"*. Property tile: *"pale cream glaze with a small [cabin] silhouette embossed"*.
- Medallion: prefix + *"a round gold medallion set into dark grout, engraved with the number [500], brushed finish"*.
- Welcome: prefix + *"a wall mosaic of a small house made of many coloured glazed tiles, lower half complete, upper half missing tiles showing dark grout, slight three-quarter view"*, 4:5. Empty: *"a trowel and a short stack of blank cream tiles on grout"*.
Rules: same bevel phrase everywhere; glaze named in words plus a swatch via image-to-image at 0.4; reject any tile with text or room reflections.

## Ranking and pick

**1. Blueprint (pick).** The only concept where the picture, the product and the law line up: construction stages are the thresholds, and a landlord already thinks in buildings. It answers all four of Brian's asks at once: one unforgettable colour on every hero, illustration with a premium bar, motion with a reason (beams build, roofs land, windows light), and a big idea that reaches Welcome, the Lock Screen and the milestone screen without strain. Production risk is contained: one master render drives every stage, so consistency is a geometry check, not taste. Watch: the house must read at 44pt in the Live Activity; the cyan silhouette is the fallback.

**2. Tilework.** The most honest and the boldest colour field; a CPA would like it most. Second because it is a chart first and a world second: the brand is "a grid", which Brian may read as cold, and it has less to say on the Lock Screen. Hybrid: Tilework's one-tile-one-hour month strips belong in Blueprint's Records tab.

**3. Porchlight.** Warmest and most alive, and properties as places is right for this user. Third because the lamps are a costume over a bar, the part-based scene is the most engineering, and the dawn/dusk rule must be policed so light mode never looks like night. Hybrid: lit windows per property become Blueprint's Properties tab.

Render step: Blueprint in full (Today, Inbox, confirm, 500h milestone, Welcome, Live Activity) plus one Tilework Today and one Porchlight Today, so the choice is seen, not described.
