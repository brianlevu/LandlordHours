## 2.2.1 sync

Folded in from `docs/design/2.2.1-sync-backlog.md` (package K, fourth adversarial pass on
`fix/k-engine` c84a2b4, 2026-10-27). Not fixed in 2.2.

- **LOW: "Keep aside" for the held set pushes this phone's dirty settings under the household name.**
  When the household pair belongs to a set held as a question and the person answers "Not mine, keep
  them aside", the hold is released through the settings precedence (`releaseHousehold`). If this
  phone's settings are dirty (a real local edit), `.local` wins. The next push then writes this phone's
  `GoalSettings-household` / `TaxProfile-household` over the other set's pair, under this phone's id.
  The other set's phones ignore that pair, because its owner is a held-apart set there, so nothing is
  lost. But the household pair name now carries this phone's values. Candidate fix: on "aside", mark the
  pair as another set's (`householdForeign`) instead of claiming it, and carry this phone's settings in
  its mirror pair only.
- **Settings of a set kept beside another set's household pair.** The verifier's MEDIUM at caf41ea was
  fixed in 2.2. Two behaviours remain:
  - A new phone on that iCloud reads the household pair (the other set's) as the settings to follow,
    not the mirror pair of the set it ends up with.
  - `householdForeign` is never cleared, even if the two sets are later combined.

  Both leave records untouched. Until the person edits them, the settings shown can be the other set's.
