# LandlordHours 2.0 — design direction

Date 2026-10-18. Author: Fable 5.1 (design lead); not yet independently assessed. Inputs: CLAUDE.md, PRODUCT.md, 71 current-screen captures (light and dark), the auto-capture proposal and assessment, the `impeccable` critique rubric (native), `apple-design`, `emil-design-eng`, and 11 Mobbin searches. Refs are files in `refs/`; links in the index at the end.

Mode: **Operate**. The user's success is "confirm what the app noticed, see I'm on track, hand the PDF to my CPA." Expression lives in details, not decoration.

---

## 1. Audit of the current app — top 10

Ordered by what hurts the 2.0 goal most.

1. **P0 — Home cannot answer "am I on track?" in two seconds.** `22a`: a greeting, a sentence of math, three pill buttons, then a honey card with five numbers (750, 22%, 56h, 165.0h, 585h) and "Pace: Behind" as a 13pt chip. The one word that matters is the smallest. The honey (caution) surface is the hero in every state, so "warning" is the app's permanent mood.
2. **P0 — Onboarding headline truncates.** `04`, `05`, `13`: "What's your…" / "We'll customize your experience based on what yo…". Source is `"What's your\nmain goal?"`; the layout clips it at one line. First impression, default type size. Same pattern in Reports: "Renovations & Impr…", "Leasing & Tenant R…" (`71b`–`72c`).
3. **P1 — Three icon systems, none carrying meaning.** 365 Lucide refs, 23 SF Symbols, custom `LHIcon` tab glyphs, plus raster tiles on goal cards (`04`). Every list row wears a 44pt pastel tile with a thin line icon (`24a`–`24d`), colored by category: six hues on one screen, none an intent, against the project's own color rule. Lucide cannot reach the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widgets or notification actions, so 2.0 would ship two visual families by default.
4. **P1 — Copy is system-speak.** "Create the evidence container" (`31`), "Evidence draft · Ready" (`51`), "Finish your tax-ready setup" (`20a`), "We paused it when you switched profiles at Oct 18, 2026 at 6:05 AM." (`63`). Nobody says these out loud. Every card also carries a subtitle explaining itself, doubling the reading load.
5. **P1 — Track is a form nested three deep.** `51`/`57`: segment → "Today's entry" card → text field → "Evidence draft" panel → "Set details manually" accordion → chips → a disabled "Log Time" mid-screen. In 2.0 manual logging is the fallback; it should be one sheet.
6. **P1 — Navigation hides what matters.** Three tabs (Home, Properties, Track); Reports is a header icon, History a pill, Settings the avatar. Reports appears as both pill and icon on one screen (`22a`). CLAUDE.md specifies five tabs.
7. **P2 — Empty states stack and scold.** New-user Home (`20a`–`20c`): a setup checklist, a 0% honey card ("71h per week gets you back on pace") and "No entries yet" at once. Reports empty (`70`) says "Behind pace" on day one. Properties empty sells the form.
8. **P2 — Property cards show a per-property bar toward a portfolio goal.** `32a`: "62h / 750h" on each property implies each must reach 750; REPS is aggregate. Three identical stat tiles repeat on every card.
9. **P2 — Dark mode is a tinted copy.** `dashboard-add-other-work-hours-dark`: the honey card turns olive-brown, progress tracks vanish against `#1C1C1C`, the tab bubble reads navy. Five paywalls (`paywall-*-dark`) reorder the same three bullets.
10. **P2 — Decorative chrome competes with the task.** Lavender blobs and a gradient wash behind Home/Reports/Properties but not History (`24a`) or Entry Details (`25`); the scrolled header blur ghosts content (`21b`, `22b`, `34b`); arrows on every CTA; an analog clock face with 15/30/45/60 ticks for sessions that run hours (`61`); coach-mark overlays for a three-tab app (`11`, `12`).

Also: Welcome has two primaries (`01`). What works: the Reports goal switcher and dual ring (`71a`/`72a`); "scheduled 2h" in the calendar preview (`10`); the timer draft cards (`63`–`68`), the right seed for suggestion cards; intent tokens already in code.

## 2. Two directions

Both: intent-based color only; AA contrast measured in both appearances; Dynamic Type to AX3, no `lineLimit(1)` on headlines; Reduce Motion swaps every spring for a 200ms crossfade.

### Direction A — "Quiet ledger"

**Feeling.** A calm book that fills itself in while you work, and tells you in one line whether you're fine.

**Palette.** Keep violet as identity, demoted to the one action color. Warm neutral surfaces: light `#FAF8F4` / `#FFFFFF` cards; dark `#0E0E10` / `#1A1A1E`. Violet `#6A56E0` / `#9D8FF5` for primary action, selection, links. Sage `#1F8A5B` / `#6EE7B7` for confirmed and on-pace. Honey for "behind". No pastel tiles, blobs or washes. Category color becomes a 3pt leading bar in Record only.

**Type.** SF Pro for everything; New York (system serif) for exactly one element per screen: the hero number on Today and the title on Record. Tracking −2% on the serif numeral, tabular figures everywhere numbers sit in columns.

**Icons.** SF Symbols, hierarchical rendering, app-wide; retire Lucide and the custom tab glyphs. 2.0's surfaces (Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widgets, notification actions, Control Center, share extension) can only draw SF Symbols; one family makes the app feel like iOS extended. Hierarchical mode gives duotone for free and follows Dynamic Type.

**Layout grammar.** Inset grouped lists, 16pt margins, one hero per screen. Four tabs: **Today · Inbox · Record · Properties**. Manual log and timer live behind a `+` in the Today nav bar. Settings behind the avatar.

**Motion.** `.snappy` (response 0.3) for selection; `.spring(response: 0.32, dampingFraction: 0.86)` for card expand/collapse; press scale 0.97 at 120ms ease-out; hero number uses `contentTransition(.numericText())` so confirming a suggestion makes the hours count up; confirm = `checkmark` `.symbolEffect(.bounce)` + `.sensoryFeedback(.success)`. Nothing animates on launch.

**Home.** Line 1: a status chip, "On pace" (sage) or "Behind by 6h" (honey). Line 2: serif "212 h", "of 750" beside it. Line 3: a thin bar with a tick at where you should be today. Line 4: "3 to confirm", opening Inbox. That is the fold (`gentler-streak-home`, `apple-fitness-ring-dark`).

**Suggestion card.** A grouped row: source symbol (calendar / doc.text / mappin), title "Plumber at Pine Cabin", meta "Tue 9–11 · Calendar · scheduled 2h", trailing "2h". Tap expands in place: Property, Who, Hours chips and a full-width "Log 2h". Leading swipe confirms (sage); trailing swipe is "Not work" (mutes the pattern). Refs: `copilot-to-review`, `flighty-detail-enable-features`, `googlehome-inbox-swipe`.

**Inbox.** Grouped by day, newest first; confirmed rows turn sage and slide out; the count badge on the tab drops with them. Empty: "Nothing waiting."

**Notification and Live Activity.** Notification: "Plumber visit just ended" / "Log 2h at Pine Cabin?" with actions **Log 2h** and **Not work**. Live Activity compact: mappin + "Pine Cabin" leading, elapsed trailing; expanded adds Pause/Stop. Lock-screen strip in the Flighty idiom (`flighty-lockscreen-live-activity`; `flighty-dynamic-island`; `waymo-lockscreen-notification`).

**Onboarding.** Four screens, no coach marks: Welcome → one property → calendar priming → the first inbox ("We found 14 things from the last 90 days"). Notifications are asked once the first suggestion exists; location on a property page, When-In-Use first, Always after the first detected visit (`sesame-location-priming`, `worldapp-location-approximate`).

### Direction B — "Field notebook"

**Feeling.** A handsome paper logbook that writes itself; each noticed moment is a slip you stamp.

**Palette.** Evolve the identity: cream paper `#F6F1E8` / dark slate `#141416`, ink text `#1A1A2E`, violet kept as the stamp and action color, a second accent in warm ink for property identity. Honey/sage keep their meanings.

**Type.** New York for all titles and numbers, SF Pro for controls. Larger display scale (34/28) and more whitespace.

**Icons.** A bespoke filled duotone set (about 24 glyphs: house, key, wrench, receipt, calendar, pin, stamp) for in-app; SF Symbols on system surfaces. Why: an editorial feel needs a signature. Risk: a multi-week asset lane and two families across in-app and system.

**Layout grammar.** A vertical day timeline; suggestions are tickets with a perforated top edge; Record is a ledger page with ruled rows.

**Motion.** `.spring(response: 0.38, dampingFraction: 0.78)` for tickets; confirm is a stamp: scale 1.15→1.0 at damping 0.6 with `.success` haptic; dismiss tears the ticket off (`.move(edge: .top)` + opacity). Reduce Motion: crossfade and a static stamp.

**Home.** A sentence as the hero: "You're 14 hours ahead of pace." with the day strip under it (Monzo's calendar-first home, `monzo-home-calendar`).

**Suggestion / Inbox.** A stack of slips, top one expanded; stamp to confirm, tear to dismiss. Receipts show the photo on the slip (Brex, `brex-scan-receipt`).

**Notification / Live Activity.** Same content as A; the Live Activity is a ticket stub with a serif elapsed time.

**Onboarding.** Same sequence as A, with paper surfaces and the stamp introduced on screen four.

### My pick: A, with two things borrowed from B

B's paper surface and serif hero numeral. Reasons: (1) The first principle is how the user feels; landlords skew older and trust native idioms, and most 2.0 moments happen outside the app (lock screen, notifications, share sheet, widgets), where only native rendering exists. (2) The differentiator is behaviour ("it noticed for me, nothing left my phone"), not ornament; A spends the craft budget on the confirm moment and the status line. (3) System components make Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and dark mode structural, not a QA lane. (4) B's custom icon set is the kind of asset lane that produced the mascot failures.

## 3. Prototype screen sequence — "Maria's Tuesday"

Maria owns Pine Cabin (STR) and Oak Street Duplex (LTR). Light and dark.

1. **Welcome + property** — "Rental work, noticed for you." One property form.
2. **Calendar priming → first inbox** — "We found 14 things from the last 90 days." Backfill rows, each "from your calendar, dated…".
3. **Today, 7:40am** — status chip "Behind by 6h", serif 212 h of 750, pace tick, "3 to confirm".
4. **Inbox** — expand "Plumber at Pine Cabin", change Who to Spouse, Log 2h; row turns sage, Today's number ticks up.
5. **Lock Screen, 9:02am** — Live Activity: "Pine Cabin · 0:02" as the calendar event starts the timer.
6. **Notification, 11:05am** — "Plumber visit just ended. Log 2h at Pine Cabin?" → Log 2h from the lock screen.
7. **Share sheet, 12:30pm** — Home Depot receipt from Photos; the extension reads vendor/date/total; "Add to Pine Cabin".
8. **Location card, 4:15pm** — "You spent 1h 40m at Oak Street. How much was work?" with the dwell as the cap.
9. **Record + PDF preview** — entries with source and detected/confirmed dates; the PDF page in a "previewing" state with one Unlock action.
10. **Evening digest, 7pm → Today** — "2 things today may be rental work." Inbox clears; status chip reads "On pace".

## 4. Copy samples

- Status: "On pace." / "Behind by 6 hours." / "You're ahead. Nice."
- Inbox empty: "Nothing waiting. We'll ask when something looks like rental work."
- Suggestion meta: "Tue 9–11 · from your calendar · scheduled 2h"
- Expanded card: "How long were you working?" "Who did this?"
- Confirm button: "Log 2h" · Dismiss: "Not work"
- Notification: "Plumber visit just ended." "Log 2h at Pine Cabin?"
- Location card: "You were at Oak Street for 1h 40m. How much of that was work?"
- Receipt: "Home Depot, $212.40, Thursday. Add it to Pine Cabin?"
- Calendar priming: "We look for things like 'plumber', 'showing' and 'turnover' on your calendar. It stays on your phone."
- Location priming: "Want us to notice when you're at Oak Street? We only check this one address."
- Backfill: "We found 14 things from the last 90 days. Keep the ones that were work."
- Pro gate: "This is the record your accountant gets. Unlock it once, $29.99."
- Timer draft: "You started a timer at Oak Street at 4:34 and left it running. Keep 45 min?"
