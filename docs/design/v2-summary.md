# The crafted house: what v2 means, in one page

For Brian. 2026-10-24. The full rules are in `DESIGN-STANDARD.md`. The build order is in `v2-revamp-plan.md`.

## The idea

The house is the whole brand now. It is a small lit model on a page that has a time of day. Nothing else on the screen competes with it.

## What you locked, written as rules

**The house is the hero.** On Today it fills about 44% of the screen. At the biggest text sizes it still fills about 28%. The rows scroll under it. It never sits in a card.

**Dusk by day, night in dark mode.** One rig, two exposures. One key light, one cool rim, warm light inside. No noon, no moon. The palette is warm cream and timber, sage and loam, with a terracotta roof.

**The windows tell the truth.** Lit when you logged hours today. Dark when you did not. Before the walls go up, a work lamp on the slab does the same job.

**Two moves, then stillness.** The timber drops when a stage is reached. Then the windows come on. Nothing loops. Nothing wiggles while you watch.

**A native tab bar.** Five items. The last one is "Log time" with a plus. It opens the sheet and takes you back where you were.

**New colours for the page.** A dusk gradient sits behind the house. A warm pool of light sits under it. Cards get a thin edge so they read on the warm page. "Behind by 14h" has its own red. "On pace" has its own green. Only five inks may sit on the warm page, because the others fail contrast there.

**Dropped.** The blueprint grid, the tape measure, the stamp, kraft and vellum. Porch Light and The Record.

## Where the house shows up

Today and the rental page get the full house. Welcome gets it too, finished and lit. The empty Inbox shows it small. Rentals in lists get a tiny house without the lot. Records, Reports, Settings, the paywall and every sheet get none. The widget gets the tiny house.

## What stays from the first standard

Rows, cards, buttons and chips keep their sizes. The density numbers stay. The voice rules stay: short sentences, plain words, no "qualified". The iPad and Duo layout bands stay, with hero sizes added.

## What changed from the first standard

Twenty-four rules changed. The big ones: the hero is not a card, and the art never hides at large text. "This week" tiles leave Today. Section headers are plain headlines. "Log time" is a real tab. The full list is at the top of the standard.

## Two things built in, not built yet

**Your own house, per rental.** Later, each rental can have its own roof, siding, trim and door colour. The plan renders one neutral house and recolours it on the phone. That costs almost no storage. Only brand-approved swatches are offered, and every pairing is checked so it never looks kitsch. The lighting, the lot and the timber stages never change.

**Life around the house.** Later, a contractor van arrives after a repair. A guest car leaves at a turnover. People come later. Each scene plays once, lasts under 3.6 seconds, and never overlaps the timber drop. The layers, positions, sizes, triggers and caps are fixed now so the art is made once.
