# The crafted house: what v2 means, in one page

For Brian. 2026-10-24. The full rules are in `DESIGN-STANDARD.md`. The build order is in `v2-revamp-plan.md`.

A fresh reviewer read the whole thing. Verdict: ready after fixes. Every fix is now in.

## The idea

The house is the whole brand now. It is a small lit model that sits on a page with a time of day. Nothing else on the screen competes with it.

## What you locked, written as rules

**The house is the star.** On Today it fills about 44% of the screen. At the biggest text sizes it still fills about 28%. At those sizes the list scrolls up under it. It never sits in a box.

**Evening in light mode, night in dark mode.** It is one 3D scene lit two ways. The light comes low from one side, with warm light inside. There is no midday version. The colours are warm cream and wood, soft green and dark soil, with a red clay roof.

**The windows tell the truth.** They are lit when you logged hours today. They are dark when you did not. Before the walls go up, a work lamp on the floor does the same job.

**Two moves, then stillness.** The timber drops once, when the frame goes up. Then the windows come on. Nothing loops. Nothing wiggles while you watch.

**A normal iPhone tab bar.** It has five items. The last one is "Log time" with a plus. It opens the log screen and takes you back where you were.

**New colours for the page.** A soft evening fade sits behind the house. A warm pool of light sits under it. Boxes on that page get a thin edge so they stand out. "Behind by 14h" has its own red. "On pace" has its own green. Only five text colours are allowed on the warm page. The others are too hard to read there.

**Dropped.** The blueprint grid, the tape measure, the stamp and the paper textures. The Porch Light and The Record looks are gone too.

## Where the house shows up

Today and each rental's page show the full house. Welcome shows it too, finished and lit. The empty Inbox shows it small. The rentals list shows a tiny house with no yard. Records, Reports, Settings and the upgrade screen show no house. The widget shows the tiny house.

The launch screen now matches the evening page. It no longer flashes blue first.

## What stays from the first version

Rows, boxes, buttons and filters keep their sizes. The rules on how much fits on a screen stay. The writing rules stay: short sentences, plain words, no "qualified". The iPad and folding-phone layouts stay, with house sizes added.

## What changed from the first version

Twenty-four rules changed. The big ones: the house is not in a box, and it never hides at large text. The "This week" tiles leave Today. Section titles are plain bold text. "Log time" is a real tab. The full list is at the top of the standard.

## Three risks you should know about

**The house pictures are too big for now.** They take 18 MB and the limit is 14 MB. A smaller file format fixes it before anything ships.

**At night, with the lights off, the house only just reads.** The finished and half-built houses barely pass. The framing stages pass easily. A better art pass is planned.

**Your own house colours are untested.** Nobody has tried recolouring the house yet. A small test comes first, before any of it is built.

## Two things built in, not built yet

**Your own house, per rental.** Later, each rental can have its own roof, wall, trim and door colour. The plan makes one grey house and colours it on the phone. That costs almost no space. You only pick from colours we chose. Each pairing must pass a check before we offer it. The lighting, the yard and the wood frame never change.

**Life around the house.** Later, a contractor van shows up after a repair. A guest car leaves after a turnover. People come later. Each scene plays once and lasts under four seconds. It never plays during the timber drop. It never covers the number, on any phone size.
