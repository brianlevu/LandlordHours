# LandlordHours 2.0 — three creative directions on the hi-fi baseline

Date 2026-10-18. Design only; no repo changes, no simulator. Baseline = `hifi-frames-r4` + `refs-hifi/SPEC.md`; its layout, hierarchy, copy and confirm-first honesty are kept. Refs with Mobbin links: `creative/refs/REFS.md` (28 images).

**Kept in all three:** SF Pro, large titles, white cards on grey canvas, the hero-card anatomy, Flighty-style inbox rows, confirm sheet, Liquid Glass tab bar, action violet `#5B4CDB` / `#A99BFF`, status on words, privacy footer.

**Why the baseline reads generic:** every tile is a grey or pastel square with a thin symbol, color only means "source", and nothing on screen says *rental property* or *750 hours*. Each direction adds one layer: icons with meaning, a category color system, a property identity, one signature data graphic.

**Shared decisions:**
- Source badges become **provenance stamps**: graphite, shape-coded, never colored. A source is "where this came from", not a category. This frees every hue for categories and properties and removes the baseline's blue-means-calendar confusion.
- Status stays system green / orange / red on words and glyphs. Category and property colors never signal status.
- Self = action violet, Spouse = rose `#B4306A` / `#F28AB8`, used only in the split graphic and the "Who" segment, never as a tile.

## Direction 1 — "Waypoint"
**Feeling:** your year is a route across your properties; every logged hour is a stop on it.

Properties are places; calendar, location and travel are about *where*. The language borrows Apple Maps: POI circles, a route line, muted map tiles. The most literal link between pictures and product intention, in a native register.

1. **Icons.** Apple Maps POI style: filled circle in the category's deep tone, white SF Symbol at `.medium`. 40pt in rows, 20pt in chips, 28pt on the Live Activity, 16pt dot in the Dynamic Island. Dark mode: bright-tone circle with a `#1C1C1E` glyph. Scales freely; WidgetKit monochrome fallback is the symbol alone.
2. **Palette.** The shared table; white glyphs pass 5.5:1 or better on every circle. Rows never tint the whole card.
3. **Property identity.** A **map tile**: `MKMapSnapshotter`, muted style, zoomed on the address, desaturated 60%, radius 10pt, with a 14pt pin in the property color holding a one-letter monogram. Property colors from a 6-swatch subset (Cobalt, Teal, Plum, Rust, Olive, Forest); collisions with categories are tolerated because categories are leading circles and properties are trailing tiles or color bars. Cards: 72pt tile; rows: 40pt trailing; Live Activity: 44pt leading, replacing the house glyph; Properties tab: one muted map with all pins.
4. **Source badges.** Pin shapes in graphite `#6B6F7B` / `#9DA2AE`: Calendar = square pin, Receipt = notched ticket pin, Location = teardrop, Manual = pencil pin, Siri = waveform pin. 16pt in eyebrows; a confirmed record adds a 10pt green check at the pin's foot.
5. **Data graphics.** The progress bar becomes a **route line**: 8pt, Jan 1 to Dec 31, with waypoint ticks at 100h (MP test 3), 500h (MP test 1) and 750h (REPS). Fill = logged hours in status color; a hollow "you are here" dot; a dashed grey marker where pace says you should be, so "18h ahead" is the visible gap. Below it a **52-week heat strip** (4pt squares, violet in four steps) replaces the sparkline. 50% rule: a two-lane bar (rental in violet, other work in grey) with a 50% midline. Self / Spouse: two dots on the same route, violet and rose, totals beneath.
6. **Illustration.** Isometric map-tile vignettes, no people: a cabin on a tile, a duplex with a parked car, receipts beside a key. FLUX prompt: *"isometric vector illustration of a small [subject] on a square floating map tile, flat shading, soft shadow below, muted teal and cobalt roads, white background, no text, no people, no characters, clean edges, centered, 1:1"*. Consistency: one seed family, same skeleton, same floating-tile base, 30° isometric, transparent PNG with the same 8% shadow. Three needed: empty Today, empty Inbox, Welcome (three tiles in a row).
7. **Motion.** Confirm: the source pin **drops** onto the row (spring 0.32 / 0.86), the route line draws forward by the new hours, `.numericText()` on the number, haptic `.success`. Milestones: when the dot crosses a tick it raises a small flag and the status sentence crossfades ("500h, material participation met"). Reduce Motion: crossfades only.
8. **Changes most:** Today, Properties (becomes a map), Live Activity. Inbox and Records change least.
   - *Today:* hero card with route line, ticks, dot and pace marker; heat strip under it; "To confirm" row with a Rust wrench circle leading and a Pine Cabin map tile trailing; metric cards show the two-lane bar and the Self/Spouse dots.
   - *Inbox:* intro card gets a small isometric tile; rows = category circle + graphite pin in the eyebrow + hours + map thumbnail trailing.
   - *Records:* category circle leading, map thumbnail trailing, pin + green check for confirmed; month header keeps the total.
   - *Welcome:* three floating map tiles (cabin, duplex, apartment) above the headline; the three bullets get Cobalt, Forest and Ink circles instead of outline symbols.

## Direction 2 — "Clayhouse"
**Feeling:** a toolbox you enjoy opening; soft, physical, friendly, never childish.

The nearest return to the original Tiimo-inspired identity, made premium with real objects. Refs: Tiimo's tiles and ring, (Not Boring)'s object icons, Airbnb's and Revolut's 3D empty states.

1. **Icons.** **Soft-clay 3D objects**, one per category, rendered once at 256px and shipped as asset-catalog images (light and dark): wrench, paint roller, spray bottle, key, two speech bubbles, ledger, small car, clipboard, sealed folder, cardboard box, two-storey building. Each sits in a 40pt squircle washed in its category tone; the object is matte clay in the deep tone with a lighter top face. Chips reuse the image at 20pt (checked at 2× before accepting). Lock Screen and Dynamic Island cannot carry clay, so each category also maps to the shared SF Symbol in a filled circle, designed as a matching silhouette pair.
2. **Palette.** The shared table: object = deep tone, tile = wash, label stays primary text.
3. **Property identity.** A **clay house avatar** in the property color, four shapes by property type (house, duplex, cabin, apartment block): 56pt on cards, 36pt on rows, 32pt on the Live Activity. Properties tab becomes a 2-up grid of cards with a house object and a mini ring.
4. **Source badges.** Small embossed **clay chips** in neutral grey (`#D7D9DF` / `#3A3C44`) with the glyph pressed in (calendar grid, receipt, location arrow, pencil, waveform). 16pt in eyebrows, 24pt in the sheet header.
5. **Data graphics.** The hero becomes a **12-segment ring** (one per month, 18pt stroke) around the big number; filled segments in status color, the current month pulses once on appear. Pace: a clay flag on the ring at the expected position. 50% rule: a half-gauge with the needle at 64% and a tick at 50%. Self / Spouse: the baseline's dual ring, outer violet, inner rose. Weekly: seven clay pebbles sized by hours, three sizes max.
6. **Illustration.** Same clay world at scene scale: wrench and key on a lavender floor; a receipt curling out of a box; a cabin with a sun. Objects only, no faces, no mascots (Brian's law). FLUX prompt: *"soft matte clay 3D render of a [subject], pastel [hue], rounded edges, studio softbox light from top-left, subtle ambient occlusion, floating above a plain white background, no text, no characters, no faces, product-shot framing, centered, 1:1"*. Consistency: one seed per batch, identical lighting sentence, 3/4 camera, matte to transparent PNG, normalize object height to 70% of canvas. Icons use "icon of a [subject], single object, no scene".
7. **Motion.** Press: the tile squishes (scale 0.94, 120ms) and springs back. Confirm: the tile hops 6pt, the ring's current segment inflates 2pt and settles, haptic `.success`, number counts up. Milestones: the ring completes with one soft overshoot (damping 0.8) and a 600ms glow, no confetti. Reduce Motion: crossfade only.
8. **Changes most:** Today (ring, pebbles, house avatars), Properties (grid), Welcome and empty states (clay scenes).
   - *Today:* ring hero with "236 of 750h" inside, status sentence below, clay flag at pace; "To confirm" row with a wrench tile and a Pine Cabin house avatar; metric cards: half-gauge and dual ring.
   - *Inbox:* intro card with a clay calendar-and-receipt pair; rows with clay tiles and grey chips; "Review 1" unchanged.
   - *Records:* clay tiles leading; 24pt house avatar before the property name; grey chip + "confirmed".
   - *Welcome:* a clay cabin, key and ledger floating above the headline; bullets get clay chips.

## Direction 3 — "Inkwell"
**Feeling:** a careful ledger kept in two inks; quiet, exact, a little literary.

"Mercury with a personality": expression from **duotone glyphs** and a ruled, tally-mark data language, not color fields. Refs: Things 3's bold glyphs, Gentler Streak's annotations, Streaks' tallies.

1. **Icons.** SF Symbols in `.palette` rendering: primary layer ink (`#1A1A2E` / `#F2F2F7`), secondary layer the category tone (wrench head ink, handle Rust; key bow ink, teeth Plum). No tile in rows (Things 3): 24pt glyph on a 40pt frame; chips 18pt; Live Activity uses the ink layer in white at 28pt; Dynamic Island shows the tone as a 10pt dot beside the timer. Scales cleanly because the secondary layer is always the larger shape.
2. **Palette.** The shared table, used only as the second ink and as a 3pt rule under the category label in the sheet. No washes.
3. **Property identity.** A **monogram seal**: 36pt circle, 1.5pt outline in the property color, two-letter monogram in the same color (6.4:1 or better on every swatch); 56pt on cards with the address set like a letterhead; 28pt on the Live Activity. Properties tab: plain list with seals and an hours tally.
4. **Source badges.** **Postmark stamps**: 1pt graphite rounded-rect, 18×14pt, glyph inside, source word in 11pt uppercase beside it. Confirmed records get a green "LOGGED" stamp overlapping at 6°, the only non-neutral mark in the row.
5. **Data graphics.** The hero becomes a **ruled hour strip**: 75 hairline ticks (10h each), ink fill to the logged total, heavier ticks every 100h, 500h and 750h labelled in `.caption2`; pace is a triangle under the strip at the expected tick. 50% rule: a two-column tally (rental | other) with the ratio in `.title2` between. Self / Spouse: two stacked tally rows in violet and rose. Weekly: seven ink tallies with a dotted last-week line behind.
6. **Illustration.** **Two-ink line drawings**: a cabin, a keyring, a ledger with a pen, a receipt in an envelope. FLUX prompt: *"minimal line illustration of a [subject], single continuous black ink outline, one flat accent fill in [hue], no shading, no gradients, generous white space, white background, no text, no people, editorial spot illustration, centered, 1:1"*. Consistency: same stroke phrase and accent phrase, one seed family, vectorize the keepers so stroke weight normalizes to 2pt at 120pt art height.
7. **Motion.** Confirm: the LOGGED stamp **presses on** (scale 1.2 to 1.0, 180ms ease-out, 2pt settle), haptic `.rigid`, the next ticks fill left-to-right over 400ms like a pen stroke. Milestones: the tick label underlines itself with a stroke-draw; no bounce anywhere. Reduce Motion: stamp appears without scale.
8. **Changes most:** Today (strip, tallies), Records (stamps, seals, no tiles), Welcome (line drawings). Inbox changes least.
   - *Today:* ruled strip with pace triangle; metric cards as tallies; "To confirm" row with a duotone wrench and a Pine Cabin seal.
   - *Inbox:* rows lose the tile; duotone glyph leading, postmark in the eyebrow, hours column as before.
   - *Records:* duotone glyph, 24pt seal before the property name, postmark + green LOGGED; month header gets a short rule.
   - *Welcome:* one two-ink cabin drawing above the headline; bullets get duotone glyphs.

## Ranking and pick

1. **Waypoint** (pick) · 2. **Clayhouse** · 3. **Inkwell**

**Why Waypoint.** The only direction where the graphics *explain the product*: the route line makes "212 of 750, 18h ahead" a picture a landlord reads without the sentence, pins make provenance visible, map tiles make properties places, not rows. It uses a native language (Apple Maps POI, MapKit snapshots), so it reads expensive and trustworthy with no art pipeline to keep consistent; the only generated assets are three isometric vignettes. The waypoint ticks also surface the two Material Participation thresholds the baseline hid. Risks: the Properties map must stay muted or it turns into a travel app; snapshots need caching so rows never flash blank.

**Why not Clayhouse first.** Warmest and closest to the original identity; Brian may like it most on first look. But it carries the most production risk: 11 objects, 4 houses and 3 scenes, each in light and dark plus a monochrome twin, all held consistent through generation. One off-model wrench and the app reads "cheap", Brian's word for the prototype. Hold it to merge *into* Waypoint (clay scenes for Welcome and empty states) if the renders prove consistent.

**Why Inkwell last.** Beautiful and safest for a tax tool, but it answers Brian's note least: still mostly neutral, expressive only on a close look. Right if the baseline had been called "busy", not "generic".

**Hybrid for the render step:** Waypoint's system with Inkwell's postmark stamps for source and confirmed state. Both are graphite and shape-coded, and the LOGGED press is the best confirm moment of the three.
