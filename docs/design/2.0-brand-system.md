# LandlordHours 2.0 — Blueprint brand system

2026-10-19. Designer's proposal. Status: first visual gate FAILED on seven measured items; all fixed in this revision (dark wordmark, tick contrast, icon edge line, phone token table, dimension rule, dark action/drafting separation, hero halo, icon centring, black/photo wallpapers), `qa/` re-captured. Awaiting re-gate. Board: `index.html`. Nothing in `Sources/` was touched.

## 1. Personality

**Honest. Crafted. Earned.**

Promise: *Every confirmed hour builds something you can stand behind.*

The house is a builder's pride, not a game piece. Nothing on screen shows an hour that was not confirmed; every celebration is a construction stage that the records justify; the PDF stays plain. The brand sounds like a good contractor: short sentences, says what was done, never "qualified" (the CPA's word).

## 2. Colour

**Decision: blueprint blue becomes the brand and the light-mode action colour. Violet is retired.**

Why not keep violet as the action colour: violet `#5B4CDB` sits 1.5:1 from blueprint `#1E3FA6`, a hue neighbour, so a violet "Review 14" pill under a blueprint hero reads as a mismatched blue rather than a second colour (visible in the r6 concept frames). One blue, owned completely, is the stronger brand; the warm materials of the house (timber, terracotta, plaster, brass) give the palette its second voice.

Dark mode flips the metaphor: the canvas becomes the blueprint paper (`#0B1230`) and the action colour becomes the drafting cyan `#8FD3FF`, the same ink that draws the unbuilt storeys. Cyan cannot be the light action (1.6:1 on white), so light uses blueprint blue, dark uses cyan: "the action colour is always the ink on the paper." So that a tappable control and decorative linework never share a hex in dark mode, dark linework and drafting labels on cards step down one tone to `#60A8E6`; text on the theme-fixed blueprint hero (tick numerals, eyebrow) stays `#8FD3FF` at 5.6:1 (7.2:1 on canvas, 3.6:1 on blueprint, 1.57 from the action cyan).

## 3. Typography

All options keep **SF Pro** for body/UI, **SF Pro Rounded** for the big tabular numbers, and **SF Mono** (`design: .monospaced`) for the drafting labels: eyebrows, scale ticks, "2026 · REPS GOAL", at 11pt, +12% tracking, uppercase. They differ in the display face at 28pt and up.

1. **SF only.** Zero cost and zero signature; Welcome and the milestone have nothing that says LandlordHours except the house.
2. **SF Pro + New York** (Apple system serif, `design: .serif`). Handsome and free, native Dynamic Type, but it is the voice of Apple Books and News: reads "Apple", not "us". The fallback if the custom font fails to register.
3. **SF Pro + Fraunces** (Undercase Type, SIL OFL 1.1, on Google Fonts). A soft old-style with optical sizes and a SOFT axis; warm and slightly hand-made, the way timber reads against cold cyan linework. **Pick.**

Rejected: DM Serif Display (no optical sizes), Instrument Serif (ubiquitous), slabs (hardware-store).

Scale (iOS points, all relative to Dynamic Type): display 34/500 Fraunces opsz 144 SOFT 50 (Welcome, milestones) · largeTitle 34/500 Fraunces opsz 96 (tab titles) · heroNumber 56/700 SF Rounded tabular, −2% tracking · metric 24/700 SF Rounded · title2 22/600 · headline 17/600 · body 17/400 · subheadline 15/400 · caption 12/400 · drafting 11/500 SF Mono caps · button 17/600. Fraunces is never used in lists or under 28pt.

Licensing: Fraunces OFL 1.1 permits bundling and App Store distribution; ship the licence text in the app's licences screen, bundle the variable TTF (~400 KB), register it in `UIAppFonts`, and use `Font.custom("Fraunces", size:, relativeTo:)` so it scales. SF Pro, SF Pro Rounded, SF Mono and New York are Apple system fonts licensed for Apple platforms only; the board loads Fraunces from Google Fonts, the app must not download fonts at runtime.

## 4. Icon language

Candidates: (A) the eleven 3D tool renders, (B) SF Symbols in blueprint tiles, (C) custom drafting line icons.

**Recommendation: two tiers, A for identity and B for function, and nothing else.**

- Category rows, Inbox, the category picker and the milestone share get the tool renders on a plaster tile at 36–48pt. Same matte materials as the house, so a row and the hero are one world. They must be re-made from the Blender material set (timber, brass, slate, plaster) so they match the house exactly; the fal renders on the board are stand-ins.
- Everything that must scale, tint or sit on a system surface uses SF Symbols: tab bar, toolbars, badges, Settings, the Live Activity, Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets. Symbols are free, carry weight and hierarchical/multicolour rendering, follow Dynamic Type, and are what the system draws in accented widget mode and in the tinted/clear app-icon modes of iOS 26. A 3D bitmap can do none of that; it is unreadable under 28pt and impossible in the Dynamic Island.
- The Lucide package is removed; its uses map one-to-one to symbols. Custom drafting icons (C) are rejected: the drafting cue is already carried by the cyan linework on the house, and a third hand-drawn set adds maintenance with no weight axis or accented mode.
- Lock Screen and widgets: the 10pt Dynamic Island glyph and the small-widget house are the mono version of mark B (`logo/mark-b-mono.svg`), which is a template image; the built/unbuilt split can be driven by a mask in code. The medium widget uses the real house render at its current stage.

## 5. Logo and app icon

Three concepts, all produced as real files and verified with `scripts/verify_icon.py --platform ios` (a copy of the app-icon-design skill's checker; output in `qa/verify-icons.txt`: full-bleed 1024, opaque, no baked corners, content inside the squircle; all four files pass, and the canvas edges are pure blueprint blue). Mark B is optically centred (bbox centre on the canvas centre, `translate(10 -11.5)` in the SVGs):

**B · Half drawn, half built** (`icon/icon-b-1024.png`, `logo/mark-b.svg`, `logo/mark-b-mono.svg`, `logo/lockup-b.svg`). A house elevation on blueprint blue: foundation, left wall, left roof slope and door built in plaster, terracotta and timber; the right slope, upper right wall and window still cyan linework. The built part rises from the bottom-left, as the hero's mask rises with hours. Reads as a house at 29px, as a blueprint at 60px, as "in progress" at 120px. It is a vector, so it becomes the mono template for the Island and widgets, and four natural layers (ground, plaster, terracotta, cyan) for Icon Composer's Liquid Glass variants.

**A · The 3D house** (`icon/icon-a-1024.png` finished; `icon/icon-a2-1024.png` roof on, siding drawn). Rendered from `design/blueprint-house/blueprint_house.blend` with `scripts/render_icon.py`: house only (lot, tree, shrubs, stake hidden), no ground shadow, the hero's 3/4 ortho camera framed at 90% of the bounding box, dark-variant lighting, composited on blueprint blue with a faded drafting grid. The richest icon at 180px and the render Brian already loves. But it is a scene, not a mark: at 29px it is an orange smudge, it cannot be tinted or drawn mono, and since the same house is on the Today card the icon is a crop of the app rather than its signature.

**C · LH monogram** (`icon/icon-c-1024.png`, `logo/mark-c.svg`). L and the H's crossbar built in plaster, the second stud drafted. Clean and in the same system as B, but at 60px it reads "Lt" and a monogram suits a studio more than a utility.

Wordmark (`logo/wordmark.svg`, `logo/wordmark-dark.svg`): Fraunces 600, "Hours" in the action colour, with a draftingInk dimension line (5.6:1 on white) as the one flourish. The light SVGs use `currentColor` and `--lh-action`, so inlined they follow the theme; the `-dark` files are fixed (ink #F2F4FA, Hours #8FD3FF).

**Ranking: 1. B (pick) · 2. A · 3. C.** B is the mark and the app icon; A stays the hero in-app, on Welcome, the milestone share and the App Store listing; C survives as a secondary mark for the Settings header and the PDF footer.

Previews: `icon/preview-sizes.png` (180/120/60/40/29 on light and dark), `icon/preview-home-{a,a2,b,c}-{light,dark}.png` (60pt among neutral icons), plus `preview-home-b-black.png` and `preview-home-b-photo.png` (true black and a busy photo-like wallpaper).
