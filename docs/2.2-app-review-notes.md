## Review notes (paste)

Accounts (P24)
- There are no accounts. Records are stored on the device and in the user's own iCloud private database. There is no sign-in, no server and no login to test.
- Sign in with Apple was removed in 2.2. The app never held Apple tokens; it only ever stored the opaque user identifier. People who used it can revoke the app under Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple.
- Settings › iCloud & records › Delete all data removes everything on the device and in iCloud: the app's whole iCloud zone is deleted in one operation, then every record, setting and photo on the device. The app then starts like a new install.
- If records were deleted from iCloud on another device, the app stops syncing and asks the user whether to put this device's copy back or delete it here too. It never re-creates the deleted records on its own.

Upgrading from 1.x or 2.0
- Earlier versions kept records per sign-in. 2.2 combines them into one set automatically. When it finds two separate sets it asks one question (combine / these are my spouse's hours / not mine, keep them aside). Nothing is deleted by the update; anything not combined is listed under Settings › iCloud & records › Earlier records.

Sandbox
- No demo account is needed. Pro is a one-time purchase (non-consumable), not a subscription. Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device.

## What's new (one line, P22)

No more sign-in. Your records live on this iPhone and in your iCloud, and everything you tracked before is in one place. Delete all data now warns about your other devices; update LandlordHours on every device.
