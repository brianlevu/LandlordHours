## Review notes (paste)

LandlordHours is a log for rental work hours. It does not give tax advice.

Goal step
- On first launch the app asks which hour target the user is aiming for: 750, 500 or 100 hours. Everything else is set up around that choice.

Apple Intelligence
- The app uses only Apple's models. There is no third-party AI service.
- Log time: when the user types or says a note ("fixed the sink at Oak St, 2 hours yesterday"), the app fills in the property, kind of work, hours, date and a short description.
- Apple's on-device model reads the note first. If it can't, the app may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute on iOS 27 devices. The user can turn that off in Settings, Auto-capture, Private Cloud Compute.
- The app never invents hours. Hours are filled only when the note states them.
- Nothing is saved until the user taps Log. Every field can be changed, and Undo puts back what was there.
- Calendar text is read by the on-device model only. It never goes to Private Cloud Compute.
- No note or calendar text is sent to us or to any third party.
- To try it: tap +, then type "fixed the sink at Pine Cabin, 1 hour yesterday". Without Apple Intelligence, simple on-device rules fill what they can.

Calendar access
- Calendar access is read-only. The app never creates, edits or deletes events.
- Purpose: it reads event titles on the device to suggest rental work, like a plumber visit or a showing. The user confirms each suggestion. Nothing counts until they do.
- The permission is requested in onboarding and can be skipped. The app works without it.

Widgets
- The Home Screen widget is back in 2.0, redesigned. It shows the user's hours toward their target. It reads from the App Group and has no sign-in of its own.

Delete account
- Settings, then Account, then "Delete account and data" (for a Sign in with Apple profile) or "Delete all data" (for a local profile). It asks to confirm, then removes the profile and its records.

Paywall
- The app is free to use with one rental. A free user reaches the paywall by: adding a second rental (Properties, "Your next rental"), tapping Export (Reports, Records or Settings), tapping the unlock card on Today, or tapping the Pro card at the top of Settings.
- The billed price is the most prominent text. Restore Purchases is on the paywall.

Sandbox
- Purchases can be tested with a Sandbox Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase (non-consumable), not a subscription. Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device.
- No demo account is needed. The app has no login. Sign in with Apple is optional.

## Privacy (App Store Connect label)

No new data types in 2.0 and no new SDKs. On-device processing isn't collection. Private Cloud Compute is run by Apple, keeps nothing, and the developer never receives the note. Records stay on the device and in the user's private iCloud database, as in 1.x.
