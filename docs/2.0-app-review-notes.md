# LandlordHours 2.0 — notes for App Review

Paste the "Apple Intelligence" block into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes.
Keep it true to the code: `Sources/Services/NoteReading/` (Log time), `Sources/Services/Capture/Classifier/` (calendar), `PCCGate`.

## Apple Intelligence (paste)

LandlordHours uses only Apple's models. There is no third-party AI service.

- Log time: when you type or say a note ("fixed the sink at Oak St, 2 hours yesterday"), the app fills in the property, kind of work, hours, date and a short description. Apple's on-device model reads the note first. Only if it can't answer, and only on iOS 27 devices with Private Cloud Compute turned on (Settings → Auto-capture → Private Cloud Compute, on by default), the note goes to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Without Apple Intelligence, simple on-device rules fill what they can.
- Nothing is saved until the user taps Log. Every filled field can be changed, and "Undo" puts back what was there.
- Hours are only filled when the note states them. The property is always one of the user's own.
- Calendar suggestions use the on-device model only. Calendar data never goes to Private Cloud Compute.
- No note, calendar title or description is sent to us or to any third party.

How to see it: Log time (+) → type "fixed the sink at Pine Cabin, 1 hour yesterday". On a device without Apple Intelligence the rules fill the property, kind of work, hours and date; with Apple Intelligence a "Description for your record" also appears.

## Privacy (App Store Connect label)

No new data types. On-device processing isn't collection. Private Cloud Compute is operated by Apple, keeps nothing, and the developer never receives the note. Records stay on the device and in the user's private iCloud database, as in 1.x.
